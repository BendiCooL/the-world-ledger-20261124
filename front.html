Lead story — Middle East

Houthi drone hits Riyadh’s tallest tower

Eleven hurt as second strike in five days reaches the Saudi capital’s financial heart

Yemen’s Houthi fighters struck the tallest tower in Riyadh on Tuesday, wounding at least eleven people, Saudi Civil Defence said. The drone hit a tower in the King Abdullah Financial District that houses the headquarters of the Public Investment Fund — the second attack on the skyscraper in less than a week, after a missile strike on 19 November that caused damage but no injuries. Two Saudis, two Indians, three Nepalese and four Egyptians were among the hurt. The Saudi-led coalition called it a deliberate attack on civilians and vowed a decisive response.

Gaza

Israeli strike kills Hamas security chief in Gaza

Naeem Abu Nahel, the director of Hamas’s internal security apparatus, was among seven people killed in Israeli airstrikes on Gaza City on Tuesday, medics and police said. Two police officers were also among the dead. Strikes the day before killed at least three more, including a worker for the UN Palestinian refugee agency.

Gaza City · Reuters · 24 Nov 2026
Ukraine

Russian strike on Zaporizhzhia kills two, wounds 40

An overnight Russian attack struck an apartment building in the city of Zaporizhzhia, the regional governor said on Tuesday, killing two people and wounding forty, including four children. Rescue teams worked through the rubble as air alerts sounded again across the region.

Kyiv · AP · 24 Nov 2026
Markets

Nvidia slides as Congress probes Trump role in Huang return

Nvidia shares fell more than 5% on Tuesday, giving back part of Monday’s record 31.5% surge after the board reinstated Jensen Huang as chief executive. Lawmakers from both parties demanded answers on the White House’s role in the reversal and on the chipmaker’s exports to China. The board’s investigation into Huang is still unfinished.

New York · Reuters · 24 Nov 2026
Sport

Senegal win back Africa Cup title in court

The Court of Arbitration for Sport restored Senegal as 2025 Africa Cup of Nations champions on Tuesday, overturning the ruling that had handed the trophy to Morocco. The court backed the referee’s decision to resume January’s chaotic final in Rabat, so Senegal’s 1–0 extra-time win stands. Morocco said it would challenge the decision.

Lausanne · AP · 24 Nov 2026
Iraq

Baghdad orders takeover of Kurdish oilfields

Iraq’s prime minister ordered the federal oil ministry to run oilfields in areas under Kurdish security control, state media said on Tuesday, after an oil-smuggling inquiry at the North Oil Company — a move set to deepen tensions with the Kurdistan region.

Baghdad · Reuters · 24 Nov 2026
